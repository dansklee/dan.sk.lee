# Strategic Tradeoffs

Strategy is what you say no to. Vague strategy docs can't be used by an agent —
or a team — to settle an argument. So this file is written as **"even over"**
statements: two good things, one of which wins when they conflict.

The intake and red-team workflows read this file to decide whether a request or
a decision is aligned, misaligned, or simply outside the strategy.

> Replace the examples with your own. Four to seven statements is the useful
> range — fewer and it doesn't settle arguments, more and nothing is prioritized.

## Worked examples

Delete these once you've written your own — they exist to show the shape.

> **Depth in the core workflow even over breadth of integrations.**
> Why: our churn analysis says users leave because the main job is half-done,
> not because we're missing their fifth-favorite tool.
> Rules out: partner integration requests that don't unblock the core job.
> Changes our mind: two consecutive quarters where integration gaps are the
> top-cited loss reason in win/loss notes.

> **Time-to-first-value even over configurability.**
> Why: activation is the binding constraint on growth this year.
> Rules out: new settings, new admin surfaces, anything that adds a setup step.
> Changes our mind: activation clears target and enterprise deals start
> stalling on configuration gaps.
